# Offline executor contract

`OFFLINE_EXECUTOR_VALIDATION` may issue only a sealed offline approval, a one-use lease bound to that exact approval, one factory-issued in-memory single-process ledger, and exact-class synthetic drivers. The ledger accepts no path, storage, or filesystem capability and creates no file. Its process-local lock proves only absent-only claim, pending-claim indeterminate quarantine, replay blocking only after terminalization, and one terminalization winner; it makes no durable, filesystem, recovery, or cross-process CAS claim. `LIVE_H4_ONE_RUN` has no issuer in this closure. The production source gate is `BLOCKED_CREDENTIAL_SEAM` and returns before lease/ledger/driver. After claim, every `BaseException`-safe path attempts one rollback and one terminalization, with rollback quarantine retaining precedence; no retry is permitted. Receipts are exact bounded projections of the same issued actual result, including rollback and ledger-terminalization facts. Real Docker subprocess adaptation, filesystem production ledger storage, and live approval are future-only and belong to a separately approved successor.
